> 💡 **Tips for best results:**
> - Always attach your **reference product image** when using image-to-image mode.
> - In Midjourney, use `--ar 3:4 --v 6 --style raw` for realistic fashion photography.
> - In Stable Diffusion, use the **ControlNet** (OpenPose or Canny) to preserve model pose.
> - Add `--iw 0.8` in Midjourney to give more weight to your reference outfit image.
> - For Adobe Firefly / Canva AI, use the **"Generative Fill"** feature on just the background layer.
