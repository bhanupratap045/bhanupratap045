## 🧡 Quick Tips for Best Results

- **Image-to-image:** Upload your reference product image + paste the full prompt above. The AI will keep the outfit design and change the model and background.
- **Recommended tools:** Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Stable Diffusion (img2img), Canva AI, Kling AI, Leonardo AI
- **Always set:** Aspect ratio **3:4** | Guidance scale **7–10** | Steps **30–50**
- **Tip:** For the most accurate outfit preservation in image-to-image, keep the reference image weight/strength at **0.6–0.8**.
