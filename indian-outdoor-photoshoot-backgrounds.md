## 🧡 How to Use These Prompts

1. Copy the **Background Prompt** of your chosen theme.
2. Add it to the end of your **outfit/model prompt** as the background description.
3. Use in tools like **Midjourney**, **Adobe Firefly**, **Stable Diffusion**, **Canva AI**, or **Kling AI**.
4. For best results, also include:
   - `Outdoor fashion photoshoot`
   - `DSLR quality`
   - `High resolution`
   - `3:4 ratio`
   - `Head to toe full body`
   - `Realistic Indian model`
   - `Sharp focus, no blur`
